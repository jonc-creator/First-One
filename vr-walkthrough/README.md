# Coach House VR

A one-room virtual reality prototype: the Coach House great room, kitchen and
entry (2965 Travis Ave) built from the room sizes on sheets A2 and A3, with the
current Weatherall finish package. One HTML file, three.js with WebXR, no build
step.

## Open it

- **Desktop or phone:** open `index.html` in a browser. Drag to look, W A S D
  or arrows to walk, click the floor to jump there.
- **Meta Quest:** VR only works from a secure `https://` address, not from a
  local file. Serve this folder over HTTPS (GitHub Pages works), open that
  address in the Quest browser, and press **Enter VR**. Pull the trigger while
  pointing at the floor to teleport, right stick snaps the view 30 degrees,
  left stick walks.

## What is modeled

- Great room 13' x 39' (kitchen, dining, living) with a 9' flat ceiling over
  the kitchen and a vault rising to 12'-6" over dining and living, stained
  ridge beam.
- Entry 4' wide with the front door, open to the living room.
- Windows and the patio door where the plan puts them, Craftsman flat casing,
  5-1/2" base.
- Kitchen: L-run with a 6' sink window, slide-in range with hood, fridge with
  a cabinet over, 7' x 3' island with a seating overhang and two black pendants.
- Finishes: white skip-trowel walls, weathered medium-brown LVP, stained shaker
  cabinets, white quartz, white subway tile, black pulls.

All textures are generated in the page so nothing is downloaded except the
three.js library. Room sizes are approximate; the drawings govern.
